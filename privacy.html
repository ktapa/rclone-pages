Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, to keep a backup copy of files from the owner's home server in the owner's own Google Drive. It can reach only the files and folders it created in that Drive.

What it does with them: it copies files the owner chose from the server to the Drive; it lists and compares the files it created there, to find what changed; it replaces changed files while keeping the earlier versions; and it downloads them back to the server when the owner restores something. The files can include photos and documents that show or concern other people; they are handled the same way.

Where the data stays: on the owner's server and in the owner's Google Drive. The app keeps no other copy, and does not sell the data or give it to anyone else.

Retention and deletion: the owner decides how long the copies are kept and can delete them in Google Drive at any time. The owner can remove the app's access to the Drive at any time on their Google Account's page of connected third-party apps.
